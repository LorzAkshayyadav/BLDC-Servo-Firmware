## Layer rules

L2 (`foc/`) and L3 (`motion/`) may only reach hardware through `hal/*.h`, and
`hal/` may not call upward into either -- that boundary is what lets `foc/`
and `motion/` compile and run against a host stub on a PC.

Two files in `hal/` call upward anyway, and both are deliberate:

| File | Calls | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `isr_vectors.c` | `foc_isr()` | The vector body is register work (clearing `ADC1->ISR`, testing the watchdog flags first per risk R3) and must not live in L2 |
| `hal_safety.c` | `foc_reset_integrators()`, `motion_reset_integrators()`, `scope_log_freeze()` | Arch section 5.5 steps 3 and 6 of `hal_safe_state()`. A direct call cannot be silently absent the way a forgotten registration callback could be — omitting the integrator reset makes re-enable trip instantly, and that failure mode is worse than the layer crossing |

No other upward call is permitted. `Tools/layer_check.py` enforces exactly
these two files as the only ones allowed to include `foc/foc.h`,
`foc/scope_log.h`, or `motion/motion.h` from within `hal/`.
